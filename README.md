# Wise Sell AI

AI-powered business and social-commerce platform for businesses in Ethiopia and East Africa.

## Six AI agents

1. **AI Business Manager** — business operations, task planning, and practical business guidance.
2. **AI Sales & Marketing** — campaign planning, ad copy, product descriptions, and social-media content.
3. **Marketplace Agent** — product discovery, buyer/seller connections, and wholesale listings.
4. **Payments & Orders Agent** — order tracking, invoices, payment status, and transaction records.
5. **Business Intelligence Agent** — sales analytics, KPI dashboards, performance summaries, and forecasts.
6. **Enterprise Services Agent** — business support, partner workflows, and enterprise service requests.

## Product requirements

- Display name: **Wise Sell AI**
- Keep the existing Android application ID and Firebase project configuration unchanged during the display-name rebrand.
- Present the six agents as distinct, accessible modules from the app home screen.
- Require authentication for personal business data and protected actions.
- Connect AI responses through a secure backend; never embed private API keys in the Android client.
- Treat payments, orders, and marketplace transactions as real operations only when connected to verified backend services.

See [docs/AI_AGENTS.md](docs/AI_AGENTS.md) for the agent scope and implementation acceptance criteria.
